FAQs

Why choose DCAPL?

DCAPL is led by a CA, CS & CMA professional with expertise in strategic business advisory, finance, corporate governance, compliance, DGFT advisory, and business transformation. We believe in “Analysis Before Action” and provide practical, ethical, and result-oriented solutions.

What is a Virtual CFO?

A Virtual CFO provides strategic financial leadership without the cost of a full-time CFO. Our services include budgeting, cash flow management, MIS reporting, financial planning, profitability analysis, banking support, and business strategy.

How can DCAPL help exporters?

We assist exporters in DGFT matters, Advance Authorisation, EPCG, RoDTEP, export incentives, compliance, import-export documentation, and strategic financial planning to improve global competitiveness.

What industries do you serve?

We serve manufacturing companies, exporters, SMEs, startups, family-owned businesses, and growing enterprises across industries requiring finance, compliance, governance, and strategic advisory services.

Do you provide online consultations?

Yes. We provide both online and offline consultations. Clients across India can connect with us through video meetings, telephone, and email for advisory and professional services.

What services require SEBI registration?

Investment advisory services will be offered only after obtaining the required SEBI registration. Until then, DCAPL provides corporate advisory, financial analysis, compliance, DGFT, Virtual CFO, and business consulting services.
